Our Journey

Our History & Heritage

Every person has a history. For artists, this history is what shapes their style and what makes their art unique. Coming from a proud family of artists spanning three generations, Eca shares a piece of her soul with every brushstroke.

1990

Completed formal art school training and foundational studies.

2003

Released initial personal collection of original floral and portrait paintings.

2012

Expanded studio exhibition to feature custom art consultations and signed fine art prints.

2020

Began exploring digital art creations and cottagecore lifestyle accessories.

2022

Launched the EcaStyle studio platform to connect directly with art lovers worldwide!

Vision & Mission

My vision is to touch the world with art. Its beautiful power to heal, restore, teach, revitalize, and bring joy is something that I wish everyone to experience. My mission is to reveal the beauty of our seemingly ordinary and mundane world through extraordinary expressions of art.
